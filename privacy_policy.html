Recall: AI Voice Note

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Recall (“App”, “Service”) is provided by DOOVIC NETWORK TECHNOLOGY LIMITED (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal information when you use Recall and related services. Contact us at ultimate.translate@gmail.com with questions or requests.

2. Information We Process

The first version of Recall does not require registration or login. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes and related saved content are stored locally on your device. Recall does not provide an account-based cloud recording library in this version. Local storage does not mean that transcription and AI features operate entirely on your device; see the third-party processing section below.

When you request transcription or AI features, the relevant audio, text, prompts and context are processed to provide the result. We also process information you choose to send when contacting support. Device and application information, IP addresses, service logs, diagnostics and subscription transaction information may be processed where needed to operate, secure and support the features you use. Payment card details for Apple purchases are handled by Apple.

If you use the Apple Watch companion app, recordings, duration, highlights and their timestamps may be transferred between your paired watch and iPhone to provide recording and synchronization features. Recording data is saved locally on the recording device. Transcription and AI features may send the relevant audio or text to the service providers described below.

3. Device Permissions

Recording requires microphone access. Importing or exporting files may require access to the files or device resources you choose. Where permission is required, the App will request it. You can change permissions through device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding feature from working.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to provide recording, transcription, summaries, speaker identification and related note features; process the requests you submit; verify subscriptions; respond to support requests; diagnose errors; protect service security; and comply with legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, processing relies on providing the requested service, your consent, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Service, or applicable legal obligations, as appropriate.

5. Speech Recognition and Third-Party AI

Recall uses third-party speech recognition and AI service providers for transcription, summarization and related features. These features may transmit the audio, text, prompts and relevant context needed to fulfill your request to those providers. The providers and models used may change over time.

Before personal information is sent to a third-party speech recognition or AI service, the App explains the data being shared, the recipient and the purpose, and asks for your explicit permission. You can decline and continue to use local recording and locally stored content; features that depend on that processing will not be available without permission. Local storage of saved recordings is separate from this requested processing.

6. Voiceprints and Speaker Identification

Voiceprints are optional voice features used to recognize speakers and display clearer speaker labels in recordings. They are used for speaker identification within Recall, rather than identity verification or account authentication. Voice characteristics may be treated as biometric or sensitive information under applicable law.

Before a voiceprint is saved, Recall explains its purpose and asks for consent. Obtain the speaker’s informed consent before saving another person’s voiceprint. Voiceprint enrollment samples, derived voice features and associated speaker names are stored locally on your device, and the voiceprint library is not uploaded to our servers or third-party AI providers. Matching against saved voiceprints takes place locally. Audio you separately submit for transcription is handled as described above.

You can add, manage and delete voiceprints in Settings. This feature is optional; declining to save a voiceprint does not prevent ordinary recording. Speaker labels may be inaccurate and should be checked before relying on them.

7. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose information to service providers that support hosting, storage, speech recognition, AI processing, diagnostics, subscription management and customer support, to the extent needed for their functions. Providers that process personal information on our behalf must protect it under applicable contractual and legal requirements, including protections consistent with this policy.

We may also disclose information when you direct us to share it; where required by law or valid legal process; where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and service security; or in connection with a merger, acquisition or transfer of the business, subject to applicable safeguards and notice requirements.

8. Retention and Deletion

Your saved recordings and related content remain on your device until you delete them using the App or device controls. Content placed in a recently deleted area may remain recoverable until permanently deleted or its retention period ends. You can manage and delete saved voiceprints in Settings. Uninstalling the App, clearing its data or losing the device may result in loss of local content. Keep any exports or backups you need and manage them separately.

Information processed by us or our service providers for requested online features, support, diagnostics or purchases is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, considering service operations, security, applicable law and legal claims. You may contact ultimate.translate@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of personal information held by us. We may need to verify your request. We cannot remotely access or delete content held only on your device. Deleting content or uninstalling the App does not cancel an Apple subscription.

9. International Processing

When you use an online feature or contact support, the information sent for that purpose may be processed by service providers outside your country or region. Where applicable, we use safeguards required for international transfers. Content kept only on your device is not transferred internationally merely because it is saved in Recall.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. Contact ultimate.translate@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

You can manage device permissions in system settings and manage Apple subscriptions through your Apple account subscription settings. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

11. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and processing to help protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage guarantees absolute security. Protect your device with appropriate access controls and keep your device software up to date.

12. Other People’s Information

Record or upload information about other people only when you are authorized to do so. You are responsible for providing required notices and obtaining any consent or other lawful authorization needed for recording and processing. Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information you do not have permission to share.

13. Children’s Privacy

Recall is not directed to children. If you are below the minimum age at which you may lawfully use the Service in your location, do not use it without any legally required authorization. If you believe a child’s information has been provided unlawfully, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Service, processing practices or legal requirements. We will publish the updated policy and update its effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before material changes take effect.
