A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good
questions.

What is Recall?

Recall is an AI voice note app built around recording, transcription and summaries, helping you return to the useful parts of a conversation.

Does this website record my voice?

No. The preview on this website uses fictional sample content. It does not access your microphone, upload audio or run AI processing.

How does Recall work with Apple Watch?

Record directly on Apple Watch, or use iPhone to control the watch recording. Pause, mark important moments, and save your audio on the watch. Sync recordings and timestamped highlights to iPhone automatically when a connection is available, or use Sync Now. Transcription and AI processing take place after recording and synchronization.

How is AI used?

Recall may use third-party speech recognition and AI services to turn submitted audio into text and help create summaries. The providers and models may change as the service evolves. See our privacy policy for more information.

Should I ask before recording?

Before recording or uploading a conversation, inform the people involved and obtain any consent required by applicable law. Respect private and confidential conversations.

Can AI transcripts and summaries make mistakes?

Yes. Noise, accents and context can affect the result. Check names, numbers, decisions and other important details against the original recording.

Where can I download Recall?

Recall is being prepared for iOS. The official App Store link will be added here when it is available.
